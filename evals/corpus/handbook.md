# Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within five business days of approval. Customers must request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Approved refunds return to the original payment method.

# Shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Express shipping costs $12 and arrives within two business days. We ship to 40 countries.

# Warranty

All hardware carries a two-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is not covered.

# Data Retention

Customer records are retained for seven years after account closure, then permanently deleted. Backups are purged on the same schedule.

# Access Control

Employees receive the minimum access required for their role. Access reviews happen quarterly and departures revoke access within one hour.

# Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all staff accounts.

# Incident Response

Security incidents are triaged within one hour. Customers affected by a breach are notified within 72 hours.

# Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid leave per year. Unused days roll over up to a maximum of 10.

# Expense Claims

Expenses under $200 need no pre-approval. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

# Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require director approval.

# Onboarding

New starters complete security training in their first week. Laptop provisioning takes two business days from the signed offer.

# Procurement

Purchase orders above $10,000 require two approvers. Suppliers must be on the approved vendor list before an order is raised.

# Support Hours

Standard support runs 09:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. Premium customers have 24/7 access to the on-call engineer.

# Escalation

A P1 incident pages the on-call engineer immediately. A P2 is picked up within four hours during support hours.

# Backup Schedule

Full backups run weekly on Sunday at 02:00. Incremental backups run nightly and are retained for 35 days.

# Change Management

Production changes require a reviewed change request. Emergency changes may proceed without prior review but must be documented within 24 hours.

# Travel Policy

Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Longer flights may be booked in premium economy with manager approval.
